Live contract change

Four registered tools and one form tool. On demand the page changes a contract under a name that stays listed, the way a third-party script could after a review:

  • __replace() replaces get_quote with another description, with no gap;
  • __relabel() replaces set_alerts with other annotations, with no gap;
  • __widen() removes get_rates and registers it 600 ms later with a wider schema;
  • __remount() removes get_status and registers the same contract 600 ms later, its schema keys in another order: a component that re-renders;
  • __rewriteForm() rewrites the tooldescription of the form tool search_help.
