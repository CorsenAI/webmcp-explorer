A form an agent fills in and a person submits

One declarative WebMCP form, without toolautosubmit: when an agent calls it, the browser fills it in and waits for someone to press Send. Its fields are named query and q because the simulated provider (fixtures/mock-llm.mjs) sends both, and Chrome refuses a declarative call that names a field the form does not have (measured on Chrome 155, 2026-10-21). Used by tools/e2e/declarative-wait.mjs.
