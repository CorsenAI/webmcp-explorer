# SEO and agent discoverability

How a site's WebMCP surface relates to classic crawlability, structured data, canonicals,
internal linking, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, JavaScript rendering, duplication, indexation
and consumption by agents. **No verified findings yet.**

Building an inspector taught a great deal about WebMCP and agent behaviour on a page; it
taught nothing measurable about search engines. This corpus must be built separately, from
its own experiments, and correlated with the findings here rather than derived from them.

## What would count as evidence

- A page with a known WebMCP surface, crawled and indexed under controlled conditions, with
  before/after measurements of a named metric.
- An agent (named, versioned) consuming a page with and without a given structured signal,
  with the difference recorded.

## What not to do

Do not write here that "exposing tools improves ranking" or the opposite. Neither has been
measured by Corsen.
